TODAY'S FOCUS • DAY 1 Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

Pure Intentions

إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ

"Actions are judged only by intentions."

1

Pure Intentions

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ

"Actions are judged only by intentions."

2

Mastering Anger

Sahih Bukhari

لَا تَغْضَبْ

"Do not give in to anger."

3

Sincere Goodwill

Sahih Muslim

الدِّينُ النَّصِيحَةُ

"Faith is built on sincere goodwill."

4

Mindful Speech

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ

"Let a person speak good words or remain silent."

5

Focus & Purpose

Jami at-Tirmidhi

مِنْ حُسْنِ إِسْلَامِ الْمَرْءِ تَرْكُهُ مَا لَا يَعْنِيهِ

"Part of excellence in faith is leaving what does not concern you."

6

True Brotherhood

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

لَا يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لِأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ

"None of you truly believes until they wish for their brother what they wish for themselves."

7

Principle of Non-Harm

Sunan Ibn Majah

لَا ضَرَرَ وَلَا ضِرَارَ

"Never initiate harm and never retaliate with harm."

8

Beauty of Modesty

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

الْحَيَاءُ لَا يَأْتِي إِلَّا بِخَيْرٍ

"Modesty and humility bring nothing except goodness."

9

Power of Gentleness

Sahih Muslim

إِنَّ اللَّهَ رَفِيقٌ يُحِبُّ الرِّفْقَ

"Truly, God is Gentle and loves gentleness in every matter."

10

Everyday Kindness

Jami at-Tirmidhi

تَبَسُّمُكَ فِي وَجْهِ أَخِيكَ لَكَ صَدَقَةٌ

"Your warm smile in the face of another counts as charity."

11

Inner & Outer Purity

Sahih Muslim

الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الْإِيمَانِ

"Cleanliness and purity make up half of faith."

12

Steadfast Integrity

Sahih Muslim

قُلْ آمَنْتُ بِاللَّهِ ثُمَّ اسْتَقِمْ

"Say: I believe in God, and then stand firm and upright."

13

Lifelong Learning

Sahih Bukhari

خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

"The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it."

14

Effortless Remembrance

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ

"Glory and praise be to God; Glory be to God, the Supreme."

15

Mindful Eating

Jami at-Tirmidhi

ثُلُثٌ لِطَعَامِهِ وَثُلُثٌ لِشَرَابِهِ وَثُلُثٌ لِنَفَسِهِ

"One-third for food, one-third for drink, and one-third for easy breathing."

16

Optimism & Ease

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

يَسِّرُوا وَلَا تُعَسِّرُوا وَبَشِّرُوا وَلَا تُنَفِّرُوا

"Make things easy, not hard; spread hope, do not push people away."

17

Small Consistency

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

أَحَبُّ الْأَعْمَالِ إِلَى اللَّهِ أَدْوَمُهَا وَإِنْ قَلَّ

"The most beloved actions to God are the most consistent ones, even if small."

18

Good Assumptions

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

إِيَّاكُمْ وَالظَّنَّ فَإِنَّ الظَّنَّ أَكْذَبُ الْحَدِيثِ

"Beware of negative suspicion, for suspicion is the most deceptive talk."

19

Serving Others

Sahih Muslim

وَاللَّهُ فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ

"God continuously aids His servant as long as the servant aids their brother."

20

Gratitude to People

Sunan Abi Dawud

مَنْ لَا يَشْكُرُ النَّاسَ لَا يَشْكُرُ اللَّهَ

"Whoever does not thank people has not truly thanked God."

21

True Inner Strength

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

إِنَّمَا الشَّدِيدُ الَّذِي يَمْلِكُ نَفْسَهُ عِنْدَ الْغَضَبِ

"The strong person is the one who controls themselves in moments of anger."

22

Secret of Contentment

Sunan Ibn Majah

ازْهَدْ فِي الدُّنْيَا يُحِبَّكَ اللَّهُ وَازْهَدْ فِيمَا عِنْدَ النَّاسِ يُحِبَّكَ النَّاسُ

"Detach from worldly greed and God loves you; stop coveting what people own and people love you."

23

Integrity Over Doubt

Jami at-Tirmidhi

دَعْ مَا يَرِيبُكَ إِلَى مَا لَا يَرِيبُكَ

"Leave what fills you with doubt for what gives you clear certainty."

24

Universal Compassion

Sunan Abi Dawud & Tirmidhi

ارْحَمُوا مَنْ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَرْحَمْكُمْ مَنْ فِي السَّمَاءِ

"Show compassion to those on earth, and the One above the heavens will show compassion to you."

25

Joy of Giving

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

الْيَدُ الْعُلْيَا خَيْرٌ مِنَ الْيَدِ السُّفْلَى

"The upper, giving hand is better than the lower, receiving hand."

26

Guarding the Tongue

Sahih Bukhari

مَنْ يَضْمَنْ لِي مَا بَيْنَ لَحْيَيْهِ وَمَا بَيْنَ رِجْلَيْهِ أَضْمَنْ لَهُ الْجَنَّةَ

"Whoever guarantees to guard their tongue and modesty, I guarantee Paradise for them."

27

Caring for the Vulnerable

Sahih Bukhari

أَطْعِمُوا الْجَائِعَ وَعُودُوا الْمَرِيضَ وَفُكُّوا الْعَانِيَ

"Feed the hungry, visit the sick, and relieve those in distress."

28

Honoring Neighbors

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ فَلْيُكْرِمْ جَارَهُ

"Whoever believes in God and the Last Day, let them honor their neighbor."

29

Path of Knowledge

Sahih Muslim

مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ

"Whoever walks a path seeking beneficial knowledge, God makes easy their path to Paradise."

30

True Inner Wealth

Sahih Bukhari & Muslim

لَيْسَ الْغِنَى عَنْ كَثْرَةِ الْعَرَضِ وَلَكِنَّ الْغِنَى غِنَى النَّفْسِ

"True wealth is not abundant possessions; true wealth is contentment of the soul."

31

Taqwa & Good Character

Jami at-Tirmidhi

اتَّقِ اللَّهَ حَيْثُمَا كُنْتَ وَأَتْبِعِ السَّيِّئَةَ الْحَسَنَةَ تَمْحُهَا وَخَالِقِ النَّاسَ بِخُلُقٍ حَسَنٍ

"Be mindful of God wherever you are, follow a mistake with a good deed to wipe it out, and treat people with good character."

32

Reliance on God

Jami at-Tirmidhi

احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ

"Keep God in mind and He will protect you; when you ask, ask God; when you seek help, seek help from God."

33

Moral Courage

Sahih Muslim

مَنْ رَأَى مِنْكُمْ مُنْكَرًا فَلْيُغَيِّرْهُ بِيَدِهِ فَإِنْ لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ فَبِلِسَانِهِ فَإِنْ لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ فَبِقَلْبِهِ

"Whoever sees a wrong, let them change it by hand; if unable, by speech; if unable, by rejecting it in their heart."

34

Protecting Unity

Sahih Muslim

لَا تَحَاسَدُوا وَلَا تَبَاغَضُوا وَلَا تَدَابَرُوا وَكُونُوا عِبَادَ اللَّهِ إِخْوَانًا

"Do not envy, do not harbor hatred, do not turn away from one another; be brothers and sisters as servants of God."

35

Multiplier of Good

Sahih Muslim

مَنْ دَلَّ عَلَى خَيْرٍ فَلَهُ مِثْلُ أَجْرِ فَاعِلِهِ

"Whoever guides someone to a good deed receives a reward equal to the doer."

36

Ongoing Legacy

Sahih Muslim

إِذَا مَاتَ الْإِنْسَانُ انْقَطَعَ عَمَلُهُ إِلَّا مِنْ ثَلَاثٍ صَدَقَةٍ جَارِيَةٍ أَوْ عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهِ أَوْ وَلَدٍ صَالِحٍ يَدْعُو لَهُ

"Ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge, or a righteous child who prays for them."

37

Valuing Five Gifts

Mustadrak al-Hakim

اغْتَنِمْ خَمْسًا قَبْلَ خَمْسٍ شَبَابَكَ قَبْلَ هَرَمِكَ وَصِحَّتَكَ قَبْلَ سَقَمِكَ وَغِنَاكَ قَبْلَ فَقْرِكَ وَفَرَاغَكَ قَبْلَ شُغْلِكَ وَحَيَاتَكَ قَبْلَ مَوْتِكَ

"Treasure five gifts before five tests: youth, health, wealth, free time, and life."

38

Traveling Light

Sahih Bukhari

كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّكَ غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ

"Be in this world as though you are a visitor or a traveler passing along a road."

39

Boundless Hope

Jami at-Tirmidhi

يَا ابْنَ آدَمَ إِنَّكَ مَا دَعَوْتَنِي وَرَجَوْتَنِي غَفَرْتُ لَكَ عَلَى مَا كَانَ فِيكَ وَلَا أُبَالِي

"O child of Adam, as long as you call upon Me and hope in Me, I forgive you and do not mind."

40

Holding Firm to Sunnah

Sunan Abi Dawud & Tirmidhi

فَعَلَيْكُمْ بِسُنَّتِي عَضُّوا عَلَيْهَا بِالنَّوَاجِذِ

"Hold firmly to my prophetic way and hold onto it with steadfast devotion."
